Welcome

Scam Shield listens for the warning signs of a scam call and tells you to hang up before it’s too late. It says ‘suspicious,’ never ‘definitely a scam,’ so you always make the final call.

Put the call on speakerphone and place this device next to it. Tap Listen to this call and Shield checks the call as it happens. Phones don’t let apps hear a call directly, so use a second device (laptop, tablet, or an old phone). Shield will ask for microphone permission. Audio is not saved.

Just hung up? Paste a message or describe what the caller said, or type what you remember. You can also paste a suspicious text message.

You’ll see the words of the call as they’re spoken. Red highlights mark warning signs, each labeled, like ‘fake authority,’ ‘threat,’ or ‘urgency.’

The meter shows how suspicious the call looks. If it gets too high, the screen turns red and Shield tells you what to do next: hang up, and call back on a number you look up yourself.

Shield can say its warning out loud. Tap Test voice to check your volume, or Mute to see the warning only.
